# [205. Isomorphic Strings](https://leetcode.com/problems/isomorphic-strings/)  

### Easy level  

Given two strings <code>s</code> and <code>t</code>, *determine if they are isomorphic*.

Two strings <code>s</code> and <code>t</code> are isomorphic if the characters in <code>s</code> can be replaced to get <code>t</code>.

All occurrences of a character must be replaced with another character while preserving the order of characters. No two characters may map to the same character, but a character may map to itself. 

<br />

**Example 1:**
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> s = "egg", t = "add"
<strong>Output:</strong> true
</pre>
**Explanation:**

The strings <code>s</code> and <code>t</code> can be made identical by:

* Mapping <code>'e'</code> to <code>'a'</code>.
* Mapping <code>'g'</code> to <code>'d'</code>.  

**Example 2:**
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> s = "f11", t = "b23"
<strong>Output:</strong> false
</pre>
**Explanation:**

The strings <code>s</code> and t can not be made identical as <code>'1'</code> needs to be mapped to both <code>'2'</code> and <code>'3'</code>.

**Example 3:**
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> s = "paper", t = "title"
<strong>Output:</strong> true
</pre>  

<br />

**Constraints:**

* <code>1 <= s.length <= 5 * 10<sup>4</sup></code>
* <code>t.length == s.length</code>
* <code>s</code> and <code>t</code> consist of any valid ascii character.
